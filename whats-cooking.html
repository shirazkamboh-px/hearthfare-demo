BEFORE YOU ORDER

Real kitchens. Real neighbors.

  • Meet the cook

    Every Hearth has a person and a story behind it. Get to know who’s cooking, what they love to make, and what makes their Hearth special.

  • Pick up, or pull up a chair

    Choose what fits for you. Pick up your meal, or when dine-in is offered, pull up a chair and enjoy it at the Hearth.

  • Food details up front

    Know what you’re ordering before you order. Each Hearth shares its menu details, policies, and important food and allergy information clearly.

  • Pay securely. Keep it together

    Pay securely through Hearthfare while your food payment goes directly to the cook. Keep your orders, receipts, favorite Hearths, and past meals together in your account.
