How dine-in works

Dine-in is a request, not an instant booking. Your card is held, not charged. During Li's response hours, she has 15 minutes to accept; outside those hours, the 15 minutes start when she's next able to respond. Only once she accepts is the payment taken. If Li declines or doesn't answer in time, the hold is released and you are not charged. Four chairs at one table, so you may be sitting with neighbors you have not met yet.
