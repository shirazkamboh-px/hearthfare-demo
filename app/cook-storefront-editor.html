Meet Li

I learned birria from my grandmother in Oaxaca, standing on a crate so I could reach the pot. Eleven years ago I started cooking it for neighbors on Sundays; now it is what I do. Everything comes out of my own kitchen on 30th Street — the same one I cook my family's dinner in. If you are picking up, knock and come in. If you are staying, there are four chairs at the table and you are welcome in one of them.

Good to know before you order

Allergy notice

This food is made in a home kitchen that also handles wheat, dairy, tree nuts and shellfish. I cannot guarantee any dish is free of an allergen. If you have a serious allergy, please message me before ordering.

Cancellation policy

Cancel up to 3 hours before your pickup window for a full refund. After that I have already shopped and started cooking, so I can only offer a partial refund.

Refund policy

If something is wrong with your food, message me and I will make it right — a full or partial refund, whichever fits. Refunds are processed through Stripe back to your original card.
