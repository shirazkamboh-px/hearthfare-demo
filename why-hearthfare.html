OUR STORY

Why we built Hearthfare

Food has always had a way of bringing people together. Across our communities, talented home cooks have been making and selling food for years, often through informal, fragmented channels without a clear path to licensing or a centralized place to operate. Turning that passion into a legitimate business can feel overwhelming. From licensing and paperwork to presenting their food, selling online, and finding customers, there’s a lot to figure out before that first order.

Hearthfare was built to change that. We bring it all together in one centralized platform — helping cooks get licensed, build their Hearth, market and manage their business, reach local customers, and start selling with confidence. By creating new opportunities for local cooks and connecting neighbors through homemade food, Hearthfare helps build stronger, more connected communities.

What is a Hearth?

A ‘Hearth’ is a home cook and their licensed home kitchen — inspired by the heart of the home, where food is made, people gather, and connections are formed.

  • Behind every Hearth is a real person sharing food they are proud to make.
  • Every Hearth is county-licensed and verified by Hearthfare before it can begin accepting orders.
  • Each Hearth is unique — some offer pickup, some welcome guests to dine in, and some offer both.
  • Together, Hearths create a network of local cooks, homemade food, and stronger neighborhood connections.

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

Not just another food app. Transforming communities through real connections.

  • Real people. Real kitchens

    Behind every Hearth is a real person cooking from their own home kitchen. Discover who they are, what they love to make, and the story behind their food.

  • Verified before the first order

    Every Hearth must be county-licensed and verified by a Hearthfare Account Manager before it can open, so customers can discover local home cooking with greater confidence.

  • Designed and built for cooks

    Cooks connect their own Stripe account and receive payments directly. Hearthfare charges no transaction fee for the first 12 months, giving new Hearths time to get established and grow. After that, it’s just 3%.

  • Made for your neighborhood

    Search by Address or ZIP Code to discover Hearths close to home. Pick up a meal or, when offered, pull up a chair and connect over homecooked meals made nearby.

TRANSPARENCY YOU CAN TRUST

Simple, transparent pricing for cooks and customers.

Cooks set their own menu prices and receive payments directly through their own Stripe account. Customers see all costs before they pay, with no tipping pressure or surprises at checkout.

What you’ll see at checkout

  • The menu price, set by the cook.
  • Applicable sales tax, calculated based on the cook's location.
  • One Hearthfare platform fee, shown before you pay.
